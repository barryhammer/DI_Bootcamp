About Us

De Tarwebol is a neighborhood bakery dedicated to fresh baking, friendly service, and a classic bakery experience. Located in the center of Utrecht, we aim to bring comfort and quality to every loaf, pastry, and sweet treat.

We specialize in daily fresh breads, cakes, croissants, and coffee to enjoy in a relaxed and welcoming setting.

All items are baked as traditional, kosher, and biological/reform breads (such as sourdough and raisin-nut loaves) under strict rabbinical control (O.R.C. / Onder Rabbinale Controle).
